Who said "Do not call me Naomi, call me Mara."?

Who said it? To whom?

The answer

Naomi said it to The women of Bethlehem

Source: Ruth 1:20 · רות א׳, כ׳

The story

When Naomi returned to Bethlehem, the women asked if it was her. She asked to be called Mara, “bitter”, because her life had turned bitter.

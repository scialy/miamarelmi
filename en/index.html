Who Said It to Whom?

A free Tanakh (Hebrew Bible) trivia game for kids, families and teachers

Free game to learn and review the Tanakh: hear a verse from the Hebrew Bible and guess who said it, and to whom. For kids aged 8–12 and families. Includes Pirkei Avot and Psalms.

▶ Play now, free

How to play

  1. Hear a verse from the Tanakh, read aloud
  2. Guess who said it, and to whom
  3. Learn the story behind every verse

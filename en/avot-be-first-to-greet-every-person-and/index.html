"Be first to greet every person, and be a tail to lions rather than a head to foxes"

How does the saying end?

הֱוֵי מַקְדִּים בִּשְׁלוֹם כָּל אָדָם, …

The answer

Teaching of Rabbi Matya ben Charash

Source: Pirkei Avot 4:15 · אבות ד׳, ט״ו

The story

Better to be last among good, wise people (“lions”) than first among bad ones (“foxes”).

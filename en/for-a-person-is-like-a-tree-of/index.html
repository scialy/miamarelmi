Who said "For a person is like a tree of the field"?

Who said it? To whom?

The answer

Moses said it to The people

Source: Deuteronomy 20:19 · דברים כ׳, י״ט

The story

Moses commanded that even when besieging a city, fruit trees not be cut down. From here we learn not to destroy needlessly.

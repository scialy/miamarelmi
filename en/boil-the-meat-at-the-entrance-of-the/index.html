Who said "Boil the meat at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting"?

Who said it? To whom?

The answer

Moses said it to Aaron

Source: Leviticus 8:31 · ויקרא ח׳, ל״א

The story

During the consecration days, Moses taught Aaron and his sons to cook the offering's meat and eat it at the Tent's entrance.

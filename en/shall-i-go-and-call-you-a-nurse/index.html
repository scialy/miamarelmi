Who said "Shall I go and call you a nurse from the Hebrew women"?

Who said it? To whom?

The answer

Miriam said it to Pharaoh's daughter

Source: Exodus 2:7 · שמות ב׳, ז׳

The story

Pharaoh's daughter found the baby in the basket. His sister, watching from afar, offered to call a nurse, who was actually his mother.

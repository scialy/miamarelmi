The answer

Teaching of Rabbi Yose ben Kisma

Source: Pirkei Avot 6:9 · אבות ו׳, ט׳

The story

A man offered Rabbi Yose a fortune to move to a town without Torah. He refused: Torah is worth more than any treasure.
